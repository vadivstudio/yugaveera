03 — The Process

Six acts of
craftsmanship.

Every figurine passes through six deliberate stages before it earns the Yugaveera mark. Nothing ships until it feels worthy of the deity it honours.

Act I

Concept

Research into iconography, temple sculpture, and story — sketched into a modern silhouette.

Act II

Digital Sculpt

High-resolution 3D sculpting refines every curve, ornament, and expression.

Act III

3D Printing

Precision printing at fine layer heights captures detail traditional molds cannot.

Act IV

Finishing

No paint, no brush — every colour and gold accent comes straight from the print, so the detail stays exactly as designed.

Act V

Quality Check

Each piece is inspected against the master — flaws never leave the studio.

Act VI

Packaging

Sealed into protective, gift-ready packaging designed for the moment of unboxing.
